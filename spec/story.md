## 1. Premise and tone
The film follows one wedding day in Titas's home, from the morning turmeric to the first look and the garlands at night.
Every scene happens at the bride's house.
- **Feeling:** joyful and sunlit in the turmeric, then hushed as the day turns, and breath-held at শুভদৃষ্টি.
- **Point of view:** Titas's. The camera stays near her, and the first look is seen over her shoulder.
- **Format:** the same as Deep's film. 1080×1920, 60 fps, paced in beats (30 per second), and played in the same
  website shell (Watch / Explore / skip / pause / mute / বাংলা–English). It ends on an interactive invitation card, and Bengali is the default.
- **Length:** about 53 s (1590 beats). The soundtrack (50 s) plays once and ends as the card settles, and the last few
  seconds hold the finished card in quiet (§6.8).

## 2. Look
**Palette**, moving through the day:
| Time | Colours |
|---|---|
| Morning | turmeric yellow, marigold orange, green mango leaf, the brass of the haldi bowl |
| The turn of the day | rose and amber dusk over the verandah garlands |
| Night | the bride's red Benarasi, lamp gold, the deep maroon of the ছাদনাতলা |

The style is the same as Deep's film: painterly, with the same line weight and type.

**Settings:**
1. **The courtyard (উঠোন)**, with the verandah pillars and their marigold garlands, the তুলসী মঞ্চ, and a রাঙা alpana.
2. **The wedding courtyard (ছাদনাতলা)**, with four banana plants, an alpana under the piri, and lamps all around.

**Reused from Deep's film:** Deep and Titas (the same rig and parts), the petal and gold-dust sprites, diyas, the
heading style, and the card frame and type.

## 4. Scenes
Timings are beats (30 per second), global to the film unless marked local. The headings are the small titles at the top
of each scene, as in Deep's film. Each hotspot is an Explore stop with a short line of text.

### 4.1 গায়ে হলুদ · The turmeric (beats 0–540, 18 s)
- **0–44:** the courtyard in shade. A band of morning sun sweeps across it and catches the brass bowl of haldi resting
  among loose marigolds. The camera is close on the bowl. This is the turmeric that came with the tattva from Deep's house,
  the same that already touched him.
- **40–194:** the camera pulls back to the sunlit courtyard and its garlands while **তিতাস ও দীপ** rises from low in the
  frame with the first petals and gold dust, then *তিতাসের বাড়ি থেকে* under it. Both lift away.
- **190–236:** the heading comes in over the wide courtyard. Titas (in yellow) and her mother come in on low stools, and
  her mother picks up the bowl.
- **236–500 — গায়ে হলুদ.** The camera moves close. Her mother touches haldi to Titas's cheeks, forearms and feet.
  Women's hands reach in from the frame edge to add more. Titas laughs and dips her head. Marigold petals fall, and the
  ululation shows as rings of light.
- **466–540:** the turn of the day (§4.2).
- **Heading:** গায়ে হলুদ / *Gaye Holud*.
- **Hotspot:** the haldi bowl, on the first frame. "বরের গায়ে ছোঁয়ানো হলুদ তত্ত্বের সঙ্গে আসে কনের বাড়িতে — মা সেই হলুদ
  মেয়ের গায়ে ছুঁইয়ে দেন।" / *"The turmeric that has already touched the groom comes to the bride's home with his family's
  gifts, and her mother touches it to her."* (draft, §6.9)

### 4.2 The turn of the day (beats 466–616)
One continuous vertical move joins the morning to the night:
1. The camera rises from mother and daughter to the verandah garlands, and the courtyard warms to a rose and amber dusk.
2. At the top, over a 40-beat cross-fade, the ছাদনাতলা comes in on the same framing: its night sky, string of lights and
   mango-leaf torana.
3. The camera comes down through the banana leaves as the lamps light one by one, and Deep is waiting (his topor first).

The marigold petals keep falling across the cut, and they only change kind while none is in the air. In reduced motion
this is a plain cross-fade.

### 4.3 পিঁড়ি থেকে শুভদৃষ্টি ও মালাবদল · From the piri to the first look (beats 540–1140, 20 s)
Beats in this section are local to the scene.
- **0–76:** the descent into the ছাদনাতলা (§4.2). Deep stands at the centre on the alpana, and lamps light all around.
- **72–196 — পিঁড়ি.** Titas is carried in seated on the পিঁড়ি at shoulder height, from beyond the right of the frame to
  her place at his right. She holds two betel leaves over her face. The bearers are backlit silhouettes. There is no
  circling: the সাত পাক is left out (§5).
- **198–342 — শুভদৃষ্টি.** The piri is lowered facing him. Then we see through her eyes: her own hands, with her
  শাঁখা-পলা, hold the two betel leaves over his face, lower them and part them, and his face is there. The scene holds
  on his face with a gold glow, then on the two of them.
- **342–502 — মালাবদল.** Titas garlands Deep first, then Deep garlands Titas, as in Deep's film.
- **502–600:** a tableau of the couple in the ring of lamps. Petals and gold dust, then the petal that becomes the
  card's ornament as the card's edge rises.
- **Heading:** শুভদৃষ্টি ও মালাবদল / *Shubhodrishti & Mala Badal*.
- **Hotspot:** the betel leaves, as the piri arrives. "পিঁড়িতে বসিয়ে কনেকে আনা হয়, পান পাতায় মুখ ঢাকা — তারপর শুভদৃষ্টি।"
  / *"Seated on the piri, her face hidden by betel leaves, the bride is carried in. Then the first look."* (draft, §6.9)

### How Explore mode works here
Explore holds on each stop's object as it appears in the film, with the line of text from §4 as a caption. The stops are:
the haldi bowl (on the first frame, already in sunlight), the betel leaves (as the পিঁড়ি arrives), and the ornament at the
end of the wedding. Tapping the object continues the story.
